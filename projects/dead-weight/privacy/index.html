Dead Weight — Privacy Policy

Dead Weight is a single-player survival game. There is no account and no game server: your run lives on your phone and nowhere else. This policy explains the little the game handles, and the one thing the engine it is built with sends.

Last updated 27 October 2026

In short

  • No sign-up, no account. We do not ask for your name, email address or anything else.
  • Your run is saved on your phone only. The game sends nothing to us; we have no server for it.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no trackers.
  • The Unity engine sends Unity Technologies anonymous technical information about your device when the game starts.

Who we are

Dead Weight (“the game”, “we”, “us”) is developed by adziusmaster. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at andrzej@lechdigital.nl.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your current run in its own storage on your phone: which survivor you chose, a random number that sets up the run, and the list of choices you have made. That is all a save is; the game rebuilds the run from it when you continue. It contains nothing about you. When a run ends, the save is deleted.

None of this leaves your phone. We cannot see it, and we have no copy of it.

The Unity engine

Dead Weight is built with Unity, by Unity Technologies. The free Unity licence sends Unity anonymous technical information about the device when the game starts, such as the phone model, the Android version, the graphics hardware and the screen size, which Unity uses to improve its engine. It does not include anything you do in the game. This is why the game has permission to use the internet; the game itself makes no other connections.

Unity’s own analytics, ads and crash-reporting services are switched off. Unity describes what it collects in its player privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertisements.
  • We do not use analytics, a crash-reporting service or trackers.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we receive none.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Deleting your data

Everything the game keeps is on your phone. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android’s settings, deletes it completely. There is nothing on any server of ours to delete.

Children

Dead Weight is a survival game with violence, injury and death in it, and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. We hold none from the game; for what Unity holds, see Unity’s policy above. For any question, contact us at andrzej@lechdigital.nl.

You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy before adding any feature that uses data differently, for example online play or purchases, and revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page when we do.

Summary

No account, no server, no ads, no tracking. Your run stays on your phone. The Unity engine sends Unity anonymous device information.
