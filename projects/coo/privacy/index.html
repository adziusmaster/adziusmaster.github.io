Coo — Privacy Policy

Coo — Baby Log & Sleep is a baby tracker for parents. What you log in it is health information about a child, so this policy says exactly where it is kept today, and what will change if you choose to sign in and share it with your family.

Last updated 2 October 2026

In short

  • Coo works without an account, and everything you enter stays on your phone. We cannot see it.
  • Android backup is switched off for Coo, so your log is not copied into your Google backup.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no trackers and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • Signing in is coming soon and will be optional — only for when you choose to share your log or back it up. This policy already describes what it will store.

Who we are

Coo (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is developed by adziusmaster. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at andrzej@lechdigital.nl.

Data stored on your phone

Today, everything Coo holds is stored on your phone only:

  • the care log — feeds, diapers, sleep and pumping, with their times, amounts and notes;
  • your baby’s name, and other names you call them (kept for logging by voice later);
  • your baby’s birth date and due date;
  • your app settings — units, the kinds of entry you have hidden, theme, quiet hours and language.

It stays on the phone, and we cannot see it. Android backup is disabled for Coo, so none of it is copied into your Google backup.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertisements.
  • We do not use analytics, a crash-reporting service or trackers.
  • We do not include third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

If you sign in (coming soon)

Signing in will be optional. You will only need it when you choose to share your log with your family or back it up. Sign-in is with your Google account, through Android’s own account sheet.

We receive and keep your Google account identifier, your email address and your display name. We do not get your password, and we do not store your profile photo or any Google access token. Your sign-in session is kept on your phone in Android’s secure, Keystore-backed storage.

When sharing arrives

When you share Coo with your family, your family’s log, your baby’s profile and your household settings are stored on our server, so that the phones in your household can share them. People you invite to your household can see and add to its log.

The server is a virtual machine hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH in Nuremberg, Germany, in the EU. Data travels to and from it encrypted, over HTTPS.

Legal basis

Under the GDPR, we process your account and household data because it is needed to provide the service you ask for (Article 6(1)(b)).

Your baby’s care log is health-related data about a child. We store it on our server only with your explicit consent, which you give when you sign in to share (Article 9(2)(a)). You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account.

Data retention

Data on our server is kept until you delete your account — or, for a household’s shared log, until the household’s last parent deletes theirs.

Deleted data disappears from our backups within 14 days: daily database backups are kept for 14 days, and whole-server images for 7 days.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app, under Settings → Account → Delete account, or by email — see how to delete your Coo account.

Deleting your account removes your account, your sign-ins and sessions, and your household membership. If you are the household’s last parent, the household and its whole shared log are deleted too.

The log on your phone stays on your phone. To remove it, uninstall the app or clear its data.

Children

Coo is for parents and carers. The data is about a baby, and it is entered by the parents. Children do not use the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to receive it in a portable form, to object to how it is processed, and to withdraw your consent. To use any of these rights, contact us at andrzej@lechdigital.nl.

You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy before adding any feature that uses data differently — for example sharing, Premium or AI features — and revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page when we do.

Summary

No account needed. Your baby’s log stays on your phone, and Android backup is off. No ads, no analytics, no trackers. If you later sign in to share, your household’s log is kept on our server in Germany until you delete your account.
