Coo — Privacy Policy

Coo — Baby Log & Sleep is a baby tracker for parents. What you log in it is health information about a child, so this policy says exactly where it is kept, and what changes when you sign in and choose to share it with your family.

Last updated 23 October 2026

In short

  • Coo works without an account, and until you choose to share, everything you enter stays on your phone. We cannot see it.
  • Android backup is switched off for Coo, so your log is not copied into your Google backup.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no trackers and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • The Baby Advisor is optional. Only when you ask it something, and only after you agree, your question and a summary of your log go to Google’s Gemini AI to write the answer. We do not keep your questions or the answers.
  • Signing in is optional. If you sign in and agree to share, your family’s log is stored on our server in Germany, and only then. You can invite another parent to your family, and they see and add to the same log.

Who we are

Coo (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is developed by adziusmaster. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at andrzej@lechdigital.nl.

Data stored on your phone

Unless you choose to share, everything Coo holds is stored on your phone only:

  • the care log — feeds, diapers, sleep and pumping, with their times, amounts and notes;
  • your baby’s name, and any other names you call them;
  • your baby’s birth date and due date;
  • your app settings — units, the kinds of entry you have hidden, theme, quiet hours and language.

Without sharing, it stays on the phone, and we cannot see it. Android backup is disabled for Coo, so none of it is copied into your Google backup.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertisements.
  • We do not use analytics, a crash-reporting service or trackers.
  • We do not include third-party SDKs that collect data. Google services we use for a feature you choose (sign-in, the Advisor, purchases) are described below.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. You need it to share your log between phones and with the people in your family. Sign-in is with your Google account, through Android’s own account sheet.

We receive and keep your Google account identifier, your email address and your display name. We do not get your password, and we do not store your profile photo or any Google access token. Your sign-in session is kept on your phone in Android’s secure, Keystore-backed storage. The people in a family you belong to see your display name and email address in the family’s member list.

When you share

Before anything is uploaded, Coo asks for your permission. When you agree to share, your baby’s care log (feeds, diapers, sleep and pumping, with their times, amounts and notes), your baby’s profile and your family settings (units, time zone and the kinds of entry you have hidden) are stored on our server, so that the phones signed in to your family can share them. Everyone in your family can see the log and add to it. Your theme, quiet hours and language stay on each phone.

Inviting someone. A parent in a family can invite another parent with a link or an 8-character code. Each invitation is valid for 72 hours and can be used once. We store a scrambled form of the code (a hash), who sent it, when, and whether it was used or withdrawn, and we delete the invitation within a day after it expires. The person who joins signs in with their own Google account and gives their own permission before anything is stored for them. Everyone in the family, including the person who joins, can see the whole family log, including entries made before they joined, and add to it.

Leaving and removal. A parent can remove another member (except the person who created the family), and a member can leave a family. When you are removed or leave, the family’s log is removed from your phone; the entries you added stay with the family, because they are the family’s record of their child.

Bringing entries over. When you join a family, you can bring the entries already on your phone into it. If those entries were already shared in another family, they are copied, and you choose whether to delete the old family.

We keep a record of your permission: when you gave it, which wording you agreed to, and the app version and language you used. For 30 days the server also keeps a technical record of each change it received (an identifier, and whether it was accepted), so that a change sent twice is applied only once.

The server is a virtual machine hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH in Nuremberg, Germany, in the EU. Data travels to and from it encrypted, over HTTPS.

The Baby Advisor

The Advisor tab answers questions about your baby with the help of Google’s Gemini AI. It needs you to be signed in and sharing. Before your first question, Coo tells you what is sent and asks for your agreement; if you choose “Not now”, nothing is sent.

When you ask, the app sends to our server: your question, the earlier messages of that conversation (at most the last ten), the app’s language, your family’s time zone, and a summary of the last seven days of your log (the times, durations and amounts of feeds, sleep, diapers and pumping, without notes). Our server adds your baby’s first name and age in weeks, and sends all of this to Google’s Gemini API, which writes the answer, under the Gemini API terms. Google LLC may process this data outside the EU.

Our server does not store your questions, the summary or the answers. It keeps only a count of how many questions each account asked per day, to apply the daily limit; the count is deleted with your account. Your conversations are kept on your phone, and you can delete them in the Advisor’s history.

The answers are general information, not medical advice. If you are worried about your baby, contact your doctor; in an emergency, call 112.

Tell Coo (logging by voice or text)

“Tell Coo” lets you say or type what happened (“Maja started on the left”) instead of tapping buttons. It needs you to be signed in and sharing, and Coo asks for your agreement before the first use.

Your voice is not recorded by Coo. Speech is turned into text by your phone’s own Android speech recogniser (on the device where your phone supports it, otherwise by Google’s speech service, under your phone’s settings). Coo only receives the resulting text.

The text, at most 1,000 characters, is sent to our server with what is running at that moment (for example a feed on the left side), the last breast side, your units, the app’s language and your time zone. Before anything leaves our server, your babies’ and family members’ names, email addresses, phone numbers, links and long numbers are replaced. The server then sends it to Google’s Gemini API, which says which events the text describes; Coo’s own code works out the times and checks the values. You see every proposed entry and choose what to save; saved entries are ordinary entries in your log.

Our server does not store the text or Gemini’s answer. It keeps a count of how many times your family used Tell Coo per day, to apply the daily limit; the count is deleted with the family. Without a connection, the text is kept on your phone only, until you review or drop it.

Your baby’s avatar

You can give your baby a picture in the app: one of the built-in drawings, a photo, or (with Coo Premium) an illustration drawn from a photo.

A photo you choose, cropped as you set it, is kept on your phone and, if you share with a family, stored on our server and shown to the members of your family. For an illustration, the photo is sent to our server and on to Google’s Gemini API, which draws it; that photo is not kept, and the drawing is stored on our server and shown to your family. Choosing another picture, or none, replaces it; it is deleted with the family.

Coo Premium and purchases

Coo Premium is an optional subscription, bought and billed through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details. When you subscribe, the app sends us the purchase token Google Play gives it, and our server asks Google Play whether it is valid. We store the purchase token, the product, its status and expiry date, the family it was bought for and the account that bought it, so that everyone in your family gets Premium. This is kept while the subscription exists and is deleted with the family or the buyer’s account.

To stop paying, cancel the subscription in Google Play: deleting your Coo account does not cancel it.

Legal basis

Under the GDPR, we process your account and family data because it is needed to provide the service you ask for (Article 6(1)(b)).

Your baby’s care log is health-related data about a child. We store it on our server only with your explicit consent, which you give in the app before anything is uploaded (Article 9(2)(a)). If you choose “Not now”, nothing is uploaded and your log stays on your phone. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account.

Sending a summary of the log to Google’s Gemini API for the Advisor, what you tell Coo for logging, and a photo for an illustrated avatar, also rest on your explicit consent, given in the app before the first question (Article 9(2)(a)). Purchase records are needed to provide the subscription you bought (Article 6(1)(b)).

Data retention

Data on our server is kept until you delete your account — or, for a family’s shared log, until the family’s last parent deletes theirs.

Deleted data disappears from our backups within 14 days: daily database backups are kept for 14 days, and whole-server images for 7 days.

Technical records of individual changes are deleted after 30 days.

Invitations are deleted within a day after they expire, 72 hours after they are sent. When the last parent leaves or deletes a family, the family and its whole shared log are deleted.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app, under Settings → Account → Delete account, or by email — see how to delete your Coo account.

Deleting your account removes your account, your sign-ins and sessions, the record of your consent, and your family memberships. If you are the last parent of a family, that family and its whole shared log are deleted too. Invitations you sent are deleted.

The log on your phone stays on your phone. To remove it, uninstall the app or clear its data.

Children

Coo is for parents and carers. The data is about a baby, and it is entered by the parents. Children do not use the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to receive it in a portable form, to object to how it is processed, and to withdraw your consent. To use any of these rights, contact us at andrzej@lechdigital.nl.

You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy before adding any feature that uses data differently — for example new kinds of entries or AI features — and revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page when we do.

Summary

No account needed. Your baby’s log stays on your phone unless you choose to share, and Android backup is off. No ads, no analytics, no trackers. If you sign in and agree to share, your family’s log is kept on our server in Germany, shared with the people in your family, until you delete your account. The Baby Advisor sends your question and a summary of your log to Google’s Gemini AI only when you ask it, and only after you agree.
