Delete your Coo account

Coo — Baby Log & Sleep works without an account. If you have signed in to share your log, this is how to delete that account, and what happens to your data when you do.

In the app

Open Settings → Account → Delete account. Account features arrive with sharing, so this option appears once you have signed in.

Without the app

Email andrzej@lechdigital.nl from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, and ask us to delete your Coo account. We delete it and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • your account;
  • your sign-ins and sessions;
  • your household membership.

If you are your household’s last parent, the household and its whole shared log are deleted as well. Deleted data disappears from our backups within 14 days.

What is not deleted

The log on your phone stays on your phone. Deleting your account does not touch it. To remove it, uninstall the app or clear its data.

If other parents remain in your household, the household and its shared log stay with them.

More

The privacy policy describes everything Coo stores and why. Questions are welcome at andrzej@lechdigital.nl.
