Delete your Cult of Comfort village

Cult of Comfort makes a player for you the first time you play, and keeps your village on our server. This is how to have them deleted, and what happens when you do.

How to ask

Email andrzej@lechdigital.nl and ask us to delete your village. Tell us your cult’s name, which the game shows on the world map, and roughly when you started playing: we hold nothing that ties a village to your name or email address, so that is how we find it. We delete it and reply to confirm, within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • your player and its player key;
  • your village and the game commands stored with it;
  • the link to your Google account, if you signed in;
  • any village code issued by an earlier test version;
  • your cult’s place in the shared world.

Deleted data disappears from the server’s backup images within 7 days.

What is not deleted

What the game keeps on your phone stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Nothing else is kept.

More

The privacy policy describes everything Cult of Comfort stores and why. Questions are welcome at andrzej@lechdigital.nl.
