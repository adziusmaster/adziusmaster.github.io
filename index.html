[ 04 ]  About

The longer version

By day I’m a developer at Coolblue in Rotterdam, building backend services and AI tooling in .NET and TypeScript. Most of that work isn’t mine to publish — so this site is about everything else.

The everything else is usually something I wanted and couldn’t get. I tried to quit smoking and found that every tracker on the Play Store either cost money or sold the good parts back to me in pieces, so I wrote one that’s free and cannot phone home. I got tired of watching language models write confident, broken code, so I built a language where a separate checker has to accept the program before it runs.

There’s a pattern in the list and I’ve stopped pretending it’s an accident: the software I care most about is the software nobody has to pay for. Some of what’s here is commercial, and that’s fine — it pays for the rest. But the two apps I’d actually defend are the two that are free, and they were built for people having a worse week than I was.

Away from a keyboard: old cars, the kind with carburettors and opinions. A bass and an electric guitar I’m still not good at. Campaigns of Europa Universalis that get badly out of hand. Rock and heavy metal, loud.
