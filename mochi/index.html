Features

Everything a new baby brings, in one tap

Built for tired hands: big targets, one tap for the things you log ten times a day, and the answers on the first screen.

Tap Feed, then Left or Right, and the timer runs, with pauses and side switching. Next time, Mochi says which side to offer and starts it in one tap.

Mochi learns her rhythm

From your own log, on your phone, Mochi works out what comes next and shows it as a window, not a made-up exact time.

  • When the next feed is likely, from her own feeds at that time of day
  • The next nap from wake windows for her age, and her own (Premium)
  • At night, when she is likely back asleep, from her own wakings
  • “Why this time?” explains every guess

The whole day at a glance

The Log draws each day as one band: sleep in lavender, feeds and diapers as dots. Tap it to zoom into a few hours, and tap any entry to change it.

  • Today’s totals, compared with her usual day up to the same time
  • A sleep that runs past midnight stays one sleep
  • Log anything after the fact with its time; undo is one tap away

Stats, patterns and trends

An average day over 7, 14 or 30 days: milk per day and per bottle, feeds, sleep and diapers. Her sleep pattern day by day, noon to noon, with when her night usually starts.

  • Trends by day, week or month; tap a bar to compare it with the one before
  • A note on Today when something changed this week
  • A PDF report for the paediatrician, in seven languages

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Mochi free?

Yes. Logging feeds, sleep, diapers and pumping, the timers, stats, growth charts, night mode and sharing the log with your family are free. Premium adds personal nap times, a nap reminder, more voice logging and Advisor questions, and a baby avatar, monthly, yearly or once.

Does Mochi work offline?

Yes. The log lives on your phone and everything works without a connection. If you share it, the phones catch up with each other within seconds once they are online.

Can both parents use Mochi?

Yes. Sign in, then invite your partner, a nanny or daycare who can log, and grandparents who can only view. Everyone sees the same log.

How does Mochi know when the next feed or nap is likely?

From your own log, on your phone. After a few days it knows your baby’s usual gaps between feeds at each time of day, her wake windows and when her night usually starts, and it shows a likely window, not an exact time. “Why this time?” explains each one.

Is Mochi on iPhone?

Not yet. Mochi is an Android app on Google Play.

Which languages does Mochi speak?

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish and Dutch.

Is my baby’s data private?

Mochi has no ads and no trackers, and needs no account to start. A shared log is stored in the EU. You can export your log as a CSV file and delete your account at any time. The privacy policy has the details.

Does Mochi give medical advice?

No. The Advisor answers general questions about sleep, feeding and development using your baby’s log, but it is general information, not medical advice. Ask your doctor or midwife about anything that worries you.

Built by a parent, for the paper on the fridge

Mochi started as the piece of paper my girlfriend and I wrote every feed and diaper on, and the briefings we gave each other at night. I wanted one app that had all of it, shared between our two phones, so I built it. Our own family uses it every day. It was called Coo until November 2026.

— Andrzej Lech, Lech Digital · the story of building it

Under the hood

The phone is the source of truth: Mochi works fully offline, and when you share, the server is how phones catch up with each other, not where the log lives. Edits from different phones are ordered with hybrid logical clocks, and the server is my own, at Hetzner in Nuremberg, not a third-party backend. The predictions are plain statistics on the phone.
